Hemming & Lengthening

1–2 days standard Same-day available From $12

The humble hem is the most requested alteration in tailoring — and the easiest to get wrong. Our master tailors measure the fall from the floor for a true break, balance the front and back, then finish by blind-stitch, top-stitch or a clean roll hem depending on your fabric.

We preserve the original factory hem on denim and chinos (the gold standard of denim hemming), so your jeans keep their authentic look while fitting the way you like.

What's included

Measuring, pinning, pressing and finishing with matched thread.

Great for

Trousers, chinos, jeans, skirts, dresses, sleeves and curtains.
